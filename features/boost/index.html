EVENTS
Random events · +5% / −25%
SINCE v1.2.0

Random events replace the classic boost. During a session, an event can occur — at most one per session — and it is either a bonus or a malus.

A damaged station cuts your rate by 25% until you repair it. A powerful Pulsar adds +5% for a limited time. VIP members get better conditions on both.

How it works⌄
Step 01
Run a session
An event can appear while your 12h collection runs — never more than one per session.
Step 02
Face the event
Malus: damaged station, −25% until repaired. Bonus: powerful Pulsar, +5%.
Step 03
React
Repair for 1/4 of a session (discounted with VIP or an ad), or activate the bonus: 2h for VIP, 1h for others by watching an ad, +1h with a second ad.
Event mechanics⌄
⚡
Damaged station · −25%
Your rate drops by 25% until you repair the station.
⏱
Repair
Costs 1/4 of a session. VIP members, or watching an ad, reduce the cost.
∞
Powerful Pulsar · +5%
Lasts 2 hours for VIP, 1 hour for others by watching an ad. A second ad adds 1 hour.
♦
One event per session
Never more than one event per session, so effects never pile up.

Events stack with your rate

Event modifiers apply on top of your base rate and referral bonus. VIP no longer adds a permanent rate bonus.

Earned = Base × (1 + Referral ± Event) × Hours
Tips for events⌄
  • Repair a damaged station quickly: −25% adds up over a 12h session.
  • VIP gives the 2h bonus without an ad, and cheaper repairs.
  • Not VIP? Watch an ad for 1 hour of powerful Pulsar, and a second one for +1 hour.

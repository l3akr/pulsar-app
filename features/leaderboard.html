The leaderboard ranks the top 50 Pulsar users across three categories: current balance, number of active referrals, and total PSR earned since joining.

Your rank updates at the end of each collection session — not in real time — so it reflects everything you earned during your last completed session. Find the leaderboard from the Station menu to see your current position.

How it works⌄
Step 01
Open the leaderboard
Access it from the Station menu to see the current top 50 across all three categories.
Step 02
Check your rank
Your own position is always shown, even if you're outside the top 50.
Step 03
Climb higher
Grow your balance, refer active friends, or keep collecting to move up the rankings.
Ranking categories⌄
◈
Balance
Ranked by your current total $PSR balance — the most direct measure of your progress.
✦
Referrals
Ranked by your number of active referrals — the more friends collecting, the higher you climb.
♦
Total earned
Ranked by lifetime PSR earned since you joined, including everything already withdrawn.

Updated at the end of each session

Your position recalculates when your collection session ends — any mini-game or referral bonus earned during that session counts toward the update.

Tips to climb the rankings⌄
  • Referrals move you up fastest early on — a handful of active friends can outweigh weeks of solo collecting.
  • Play DriftScan during an active session — the bonus counts toward your rank once that session ends.
  • Even outside the top 50, your personal rank is always visible.
  • Combine VIP and Boost to grow your balance faster and climb the balance ranking.

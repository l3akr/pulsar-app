DRIFTSCAN
Arcade · Earn PSR
LIVE IN BETA

DriftScan is an arcade dodging game built directly into the Pulsar app. You pilot a spacecraft through deep space, dodge incoming asteroids, and capture pulsars to accumulate bonus PSR tokens.

Every run is capped at 1 minute by a draining fuel gauge. Asteroid hits cost you a life — lose all 3 and the run ends early. Either way, your bonus is added on top of your active collection session earnings.

How it works

⌄
Step 01
Launch the game
Access DriftScan from the Station menu. No energy cost, no entry fee. Just open and play.
Step 02
Pilot your ship
Steer your spacecraft by tilting or tapping while your fuel gauge ticks down. Dodge asteroids — each hit costs a life.
Step 03
Capture pulsars
Fly through glowing pulsar circles before your fuel runs out or your 3 lives are gone — whichever comes first.

Game elements

⌄
✦
Pulsars
Glowing circular objects floating through space. Fly through them to earn PSR. The longer you survive, the more you can collect.
◈
Asteroids
Obstacles drifting across your path. Each collision removes one life. Lose all 3 and the run ends early.
▲
Your ship
A sleek spacecraft with engine trails. The ship accelerates as you progress — reflexes get tested further into the run.
♥
Lives system
You start each run with 3 lives. Each asteroid hit removes one — lose all 3 and the run ends early, before your minute is up.
⛽
Fuel timer
A draining gauge caps every run at 1 minute. When it hits empty, the run ends and your bonus is locked in.
⏳
Plays & cooldown
Each mini-game has a limited number of runs per 12h session. Wait 5 minutes between runs, or watch an ad to jump back in immediately — VIP members wait just 1 minute, no ad needed.
+PSR

Bonus credited automatically

Your DriftScan earnings are added directly to your balance at the end of the game. They stack with your active 12h collection session and your referral bonuses — every source of PSR compounds.

Tips to maximize your bonus

⌄
  • Prioritize pulsar capture over asteroid avoidance when they overlap — a life costs less than missing a pulsar cluster.
  • Watch your fuel gauge — plan your last pulsar grab before it hits empty.
  • You get a limited number of runs per 12h session, with a 5-minute cooldown between them — watch an ad to jump back in immediately, or just 1 minute if you're VIP.
  • Combine with an active boost (+2%) to maximize your total hourly rate while playing.

DRIFTSCAN
Arcade · Earn PSR
LIVE IN BETA

DriftScan is a space shooter integrated directly into the Pulsar app. You pilot a spacecraft through deep space, dodge incoming asteroids and obstacles, and capture pulsars to accumulate bonus PSR tokens.

Every successful pulsar capture adds to your estimated reward. The game ends when you lose all 3 lives — your bonus is then added on top of your active collection session earnings.

How it works

Step 01
Launch the game
Access DriftScan from the Station menu. No energy cost, no entry fee. Just open and play.
Step 02
Pilot your ship
Steer your spacecraft by tilting or tapping. Dodge asteroids — each hit costs you one of your 3 lives.
Step 03
Capture pulsars
Fly through glowing pulsar circles to collect them. Each capture increases your PSR estimation counter.

Game elements

✦
Pulsars
Glowing circular objects floating through space. Fly through them to earn PSR. The longer you survive, the more you can collect.
◈
Asteroids
Obstacles drifting across your path. Each collision removes one life. Lose all 3 and the game ends.
▲
Your ship
A sleek spacecraft with engine trails. The ship accelerates as you progress — reflexes get tested further into the run.
♥
Lives system
You start each game with 3 lives. Each asteroid hit removes one. When all 3 are gone, your earned bonus is locked in and credited.
+PSR

Bonus credited automatically

Your DriftScan earnings are added directly to your balance at the end of the game. They stack with your active 12h collection session and your referral bonuses — every source of PSR compounds.

Tips to maximize your bonus

  • Prioritize pulsar capture over asteroid avoidance when they overlap — a life costs less than missing a pulsar cluster.
  • Stay near the center of the screen — most pulsars spawn toward the middle and asteroids tend to come from the edges.
  • Play multiple sessions per day — there is no cooldown on mini-game entries.
  • Combine with an active boost (+2%) to maximize your total hourly rate while playing.

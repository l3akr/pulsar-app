FAQ

Quick answers to everything about Pulsar. Tap a question to expand it, or filter by topic below.

General
Is Pulsar free?⌄

Yes. Downloading and using Pulsar costs nothing. VIP subscriptions are optional.

Do I need an email to sign up?⌄

No. Just pick a username and password — no email, no phone number, ever.

What is $PSR?⌄

$PSR is the token you earn by collecting inside Pulsar — through sessions, referrals, Boost and mini-games.

Collecting
How does collecting work?⌄

Start a 12h session with one tap. Your $PSR balance grows automatically at your rate until the session ends.

What determines my rate?⌄

Your base rate — set by Pulsar's total user count — plus your referral bonus, VIP boost and Boost ad bonus.

Can I start a new session right away?⌄

Yes. As soon as one session ends, you can start another immediately.

Boost
What does Boost do?⌄

Watch a rewarded ad for +2% rate for 1 hour. VIP members get the same boost with a tap, no ad required.

How many times can I boost?⌄

No cap — chain as many boosts as you want, back-to-back, as long as a session is active.

Referral
How does the referral bonus work?⌄

You get +1% rate for every friend who collects at least once every 48h. It pauses if they go inactive, and resumes when they're back.

What does my friend get?⌄

A free 12h session's worth of PSR, credited instantly when they sign up with your code.

VIP
What are the VIP tiers?⌄

Four rate boosts to choose from — +0%, +5%, +10% or +15%. Every tier removes all ads.

When does my VIP boost apply?⌄

Immediately, the moment you subscribe — no need to wait for a new session.

Mini-games
How do mini-games work?⌄

DriftScan is an arcade dodging game — capture pulsars and avoid asteroids before your 1-minute fuel gauge empties or you lose all 3 lives.

Is there a limit on how often I can play?⌄

Yes — a limited number of runs per session, with a 5-minute cooldown between them (1 minute for VIP, no ad needed).

Leaderboard
How is the leaderboard ranked?⌄

By balance, referrals or total earned — top 50 in each category, updated at the end of every session.

Privacy
Do you collect personal data?⌄

No — no email, no phone number, no personal information. Just a username and password.

What if I lose my password?⌄

It currently can't be recovered. A recovery phrase feature is planned for a future update.

Can I use my account on another device?⌄

Yes — as long as that device has never had another Pulsar account on it.

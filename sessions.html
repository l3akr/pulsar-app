A session is Pulsar's core collection mechanic. Once launched, it runs for 12 hours in the background, crediting $PSR to your balance at your effective hourly rate — no need to keep the app open.

When the timer ends, your earnings are calculated securely on the server and credited instantly. You can then start a brand new session right away.

How it works

Step 01
Start a session
Watch a short non-skippable ad (skipped automatically for VIP), then your 12-hour session begins.
Step 02
Let it run
Your $PSR balance grows automatically at your effective rate. Close the app — the session keeps running.
Step 03
Collect & restart
Tokens are credited instantly once the session ends. Start a new one immediately to keep growing.

Rate components

◈
Base rate
Set by the current milestone tier. It drops as more users join Pulsar globally, so early adopters earn more.
✦
Referral bonus
+1% rate for every friend who becomes active within 48h of signing up with your code. No cap, no expiry.
♦
VIP boost
0% to +15% depending on your subscription tier. VIP also removes all ads, including the session-launch ad.
▲
Ad boost
+2% rate for 1 hour after watching a rewarded ad, once per session. Free for VIP members.
Earned = Base × (1 + Referral + VIP + Boost) × Hours

Calculated server-side, credited automatically

Your session's earnings are computed exclusively by Firebase Cloud Functions and added to your balance the moment the 12 hours are up — nothing to claim manually.

Tips to maximize your rate

  • Start a new session as soon as the previous one ends to avoid downtime between collections.
  • Refer active friends before launching a session to lock in the +1% bonus for the full 12 hours.
  • Watch the boost ad right after starting — it stacks on top of your referral and VIP bonuses.
  • Check the milestone table below — the earlier you join, the higher your base rate.
Milestone system

Your base rate depends on total users

The base rate is fixed by the total number of registered Pulsar users, not by time. Every milestone crossed lowers the rate for new sessions — you get an instant push notification when it happens.
